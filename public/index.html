About Dunellan

Our lodge

Dunellan is a modern, progressive lodge for men of all ages who wish to follow the principles and traditions of Freemasonry, improve their own wellbeing and serve their community.

Our members

Our brethren come from many backgrounds and professions. They live across Brisbane and beyond, from the Gold Coast to Canberra and as far as Chile.

Our welcome

Visitors and friends receive a warm welcome in the Lodge Room and at the Festive Board after each meeting, where harmony and good company prevail.

Three degrees of the Craft

Every Freemason progresses through three ceremonies, called degrees.

I

Entered Apprentice

The first degree. Your first step into Freemasonry and the foundations of lodge life.

II

Fellowcraft

The second degree, focused on learning and personal growth.

III

Master Mason

The third degree and full membership of the lodge. Master Masons may progress through the lodge offices to the Master's chair.

How to join

It starts with a friendly chat to see whether Freemasonry suits you.

  1. Get in touchUse the form below or email our Secretary.
  2. Meet usJoin us at a Festive Board or social event.
  3. ApplyA member nominates you and you provide a police check.
  4. InitiationThe lodge welcomes you as a new member.

Who can join

Age
Men over the age of 18
Character
Honest, law abiding and of good character
Belief
In a Supreme Being, of any faith

Expect one lodge meeting a month, plus a joining fee and yearly dues. The Secretary will explain the details.
